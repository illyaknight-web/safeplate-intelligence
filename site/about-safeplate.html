Function Media LLC · SAFEPLATE™

Food-safety intelligence infrastructure built around evidence, provenance, and earlier context.

SAFEPLATE™ by Function Media LLC is designed to organize food-safety information across recalls, outbreak investigations, products, lots, companies, facilities, locations, hazards, and evidence while preserving where each fact came from.

The platform is intended to distinguish verified government information from emerging signals, contextual risk information, and analytical inference. Its purpose is not to manufacture certainty; it is to help people understand the evidence and relationships behind a developing food-safety situation.

SAFEPLATE™ sits within the Function Media LLC technology portfolio alongside VERISCOPE™ for high-consequence operational intelligence and NORTHLINE™ for K–12 district operations intelligence.

Function Media LLC was founded by Illya Knight, Founder and Managing Member. Shea Johnson is Co-Owner.
