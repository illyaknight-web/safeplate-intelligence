About SAFEPLATE™ by Function Media LLC

Evidence before assumption.

SAFEPLATE™ by Function Media LLC is designed to organize food-safety information across recalls, outbreak investigations, products, lots, companies, facilities, locations, hazards, and evidence while preserving where each fact came from.

01 / The premise

Food safety becomes intelligence when the evidence stays connected.

The platform is intended to distinguish verified government information from emerging signals, contextual risk information, and analytical inference.

Its purpose is not to manufacture certainty; it is to help people understand the evidence and relationships behind a developing food-safety situation.

A recall list can tell you what happened. Connected evidence can help show how the pieces relate.

Built around provenance, not prediction theater.

SAFEPLATE™ is being developed as infrastructure for accountable food-safety intelligence: organizing permitted evidence while keeping source, status, uncertainty, correction, and human review visible.
01

Connect evidence

Bring permitted recall, outbreak, facility, product, hazard, environmental, and related records into a more coherent operational view.

02

Preserve provenance

Keep source, status, corrections, conflicts, and human review visible so verified evidence is not confused with context or inference.

03

Support investigation

Help qualified users resolve entities, trace relationships, identify relevant signals, and narrow what the evidence actually supports.
