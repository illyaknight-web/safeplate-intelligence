What SAFEPLATE is designed to do

Connect evidenceBring permitted recall, outbreak, facility, product, hazard, environmental, and related records into a more coherent operational view.
Preserve provenanceKeep source, status, corrections, conflicts, and human review visible so users can distinguish verified evidence from context or inference.
Support investigationHelp qualified users resolve entities, trace relationships, identify relevant signals, and narrow what the evidence actually supports.

Built for accountable food-safety intelligence

SAFEPLATE™ is not presented as a replacement for regulators, laboratories, public-health professionals, producers, distributors, or institutional food-safety teams. The platform direction is to support those roles with better evidence organization, clearer provenance, and human-reviewed intelligence.
