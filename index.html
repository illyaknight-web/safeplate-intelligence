Recall scope

Connected ecosystem

Access follows role, not proximity.

SAFEPLATE is designed to support many participants in the food system — but not all with equal access. What a user can see and do depends on role, authorization, data-sharing agreements and applicable law.

Tier 1

Public Access

Understandable product, recall and safety information, shown where that information is lawfully available. No facility-level operational detail.

ConsumersGeneral public
Tier 2

Authorized Institutional Access

Operational tools for record connection, evidence review and coordination — available to organizations with a data agreement and authorized credentials.

Growers & producersProcessors & packersLaboratoriesDistributorsRetail & food-serviceSchools, hospitals & institutional buyers
Tier 3

Regulatory-Coordinated Access

Coordinated visibility intended for public-health and agricultural agencies and regulators, where authorization and governing law permit it. Not a substitute for a regulator's own systems or authority.

Public-health agenciesAgricultural agenciesRegulators
System layers

One system, three connected layers.

SAFEPLATE PUBLIC, SAFEPLATE INSTITUTIONAL and SAFEPLATE CORE are connected parts of one platform — not separate products.

Top layer

SAFEPLATE PUBLIC

A public-facing layer for understandable product, recall and safety information where data is lawfully available.

Middle layer

SAFEPLATE INSTITUTIONAL

Authorized operational tools for investigations, review, evidence management, coordination and decision support.

Foundation

SAFEPLATE CORE

The underlying identity resolution, Food Intelligence Graph™, Entity Resolution Engine™, AI Signal Detection Engine™, IRAM™, evidence, audit and access-control infrastructure that the Public and Institutional layers are built on.
